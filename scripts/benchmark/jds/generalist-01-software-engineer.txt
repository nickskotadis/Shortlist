Software Engineer
Pinehurst Systems — Raleigh, NC (Hybrid)

Pinehurst builds inventory and point-of-sale software for independent pharmacies. About 2,600 pharmacies use us. Our software handles prescription intake, insurance adjudication, inventory ordering, and the register.

We are hiring a Software Engineer. This is a generalist role — you will work across the stack and across products depending on what needs doing.

The work

In a given quarter you might build a feature in our web application, fix a performance problem in a database query, write an integration with a wholesaler's ordering API, or debug why a receipt printer at a pharmacy in Ohio is producing garbage output. We are a small enough team that specialization is a luxury.

Current projects on the roadmap include: rebuilding our insurance rejection handling (pharmacists spend real time on this daily and our current interface is bad), an integration with a third wholesaler, and moving our reporting off the production database where it does not belong.

What we're looking for

Two to six years of professional software engineering. We are flexible on the level and will calibrate the offer to what we see.

Competence in at least one server-side language and a willingness to work in ours, which is C# and .NET. We have hired people from Java, Python, and Ruby backgrounds and it has worked out.

SQL comfort. Our data model is the heart of the product and you will spend meaningful time in SQL Server.

Some frontend ability. Our web application is React; you do not need to be a specialist but you should be able to build a form that works.

Willingness to learn the domain. Pharmacy has a lot of specific rules — DEA scheduling, insurance rejection codes, 340B pricing — and understanding them is what separates a useful engineer here from one who ships technically correct features that do not fit how a pharmacy works.

We do not require

A computer science degree.

Healthcare or pharmacy experience.

Experience with our exact stack.

What it's like here

We are 65 people in Raleigh. Engineering is 18. Hybrid, three days in office.

Our customers are independent pharmacies, which are small businesses under real economic pressure from chains and PBMs. They call our support line and talk to a person. Engineers rotate through a support shadowing week once a year and it is the fastest education in what the product actually needs.

We ship on a two-week cadence. There is no on-call rotation for most engineers; we have a small ops team that handles after-hours, and engineers are escalated to rarely.

Compensation is $95,000 - $135,000 depending on experience, with full benefits and a 4% 401k match. We do annual raises in April and we do not make people negotiate for them.
