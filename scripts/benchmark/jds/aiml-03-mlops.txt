MLOps Engineer
Perch Insurance Technology — Remote (US)

Perch provides underwriting and claims models to regional property and casualty insurers. Twenty-two carriers use our models to price policies and triage claims. Because we operate in a regulated industry, every model we deploy has to be explainable to a state insurance regulator, auditable years after the fact, and demonstrably free of prohibited rating factors.

We are hiring an MLOps Engineer to own the infrastructure that makes that possible.

Why this is not a standard MLOps role

In most companies, model governance is a nice-to-have that gets built after something goes wrong. Here it is a precondition for shipping. A model we cannot explain is a model we cannot deploy, and a model whose training data we cannot reconstruct three years later is a compliance liability.

That means lineage, reproducibility, and audit trails are first-class engineering requirements, not paperwork bolted on afterward.

Responsibilities

Own our model registry and deployment pipeline. Every model version needs to be traceable to its exact training data, code, hyperparameters, and the person who approved it.

Build and maintain reproducible training pipelines. "Rerun this model exactly as it was trained in March 2024" needs to be a command, not an archaeology project.

Own monitoring: data drift, prediction drift, and fairness metrics computed continuously across protected-class proxies. Our regulators care about disparate impact and so do we.

Support the model risk management process. You will work with our actuarial and compliance teams to produce documentation packages for regulatory filings.

Improve iteration speed for the data science team, which currently waits too long between having a model idea and being able to evaluate it.

Requirements

Four or more years in MLOps, ML platform, or infrastructure engineering supporting ML teams.

Strong Python. Comfort with infrastructure as code (we use Terraform) and containers (Kubernetes on AWS).

Experience with an ML platform stack: MLflow, Kubeflow, SageMaker, Metaflow, or equivalent. We use MLflow and are not married to it.

Experience with data versioning and lineage. If you have opinions about the tradeoffs between DVC, LakeFS, and just being extremely disciplined with S3 paths, we want to hear them.

Ability to work with non-engineers. Actuaries are rigorous, exacting, and do not speak your vocabulary. This collaboration is a large part of the job.

Strongly preferred

Regulated industry experience: insurance, banking, healthcare, or anywhere with a model risk management function.

Familiarity with fairness metrics and their limitations.

Experience with explainability tooling (SHAP, LIME) and its limits in a regulatory conversation.

Terms

$160,000 - $195,000, fully remote in the US.

Perch is 150 people. Our pace is deliberate — we ship model updates on a quarterly cadence, not continuously, because each one goes through review. If you want to ship daily, this is the wrong company, and we say so early.
