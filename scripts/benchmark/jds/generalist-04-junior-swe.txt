Software Engineer I
Wexler Municipal Software — Sacramento, CA (Hybrid)

Wexler builds permitting, licensing, and code enforcement software for city and county governments. About 340 jurisdictions use us. If you have ever applied for a building permit online in a mid-size American city, there is a reasonable chance you used something we built.

We are hiring a Software Engineer I. This is an early-career role and we mean it — we are looking for people with zero to three years of professional experience, and we have a structured onboarding program because we hire at this level deliberately every year.

What you'd do

Work on features in our permitting product alongside a mentor for your first six months. You will have real work from week two, but you will not be alone with it.

Fix bugs. This is how most new engineers learn our codebase and we do not treat it as junior-engineer busywork — bug fixing here requires understanding why the system behaves as it does.

Write tests. We take testing seriously and code review will ask about it.

Participate in customer support escalations. Municipal staff are our users and their feedback is direct and specific.

Our stack is Java and Spring on the backend, React and TypeScript on the frontend, and Postgres. We run on AWS GovCloud.

What we're looking for

Zero to three years of professional experience. Bootcamp graduates, CS graduates, and career changers are all welcome; we hire from all three every year.

Demonstrated ability to write code. That can be a portfolio, open source contributions, class projects you can talk about in detail, or prior professional work. We care that you can walk us through something you built and explain your decisions.

Curiosity about how things work. The best predictor of success here has been people who ask why.

Willingness to work in a large existing codebase. Our system is 14 years old. Nobody's first year here involves greenfield work.

Interest in civic technology. Our customers are local governments with constrained budgets serving residents who have no alternative provider. We think that matters and we hire people who agree.

We are not looking for

Any specific technology experience. We will teach you Java.

A CS degree specifically. About 40% of our engineering team does not have one.

Leetcode performance. Our interview has no algorithm puzzle. We do a code reading exercise and a conversation about a project you have built.

The offer

$85,000 - $105,000. Hybrid in Sacramento, three days a week.

Benefits include full health coverage, a pension-style retirement contribution (unusual in tech, a legacy of our public-sector orientation), and 18 days PTO to start.

We have a formal mentorship program, a monthly internal tech talk, and a $1,500 annual education budget. Our last four Software Engineer I hires are all still here; three have been promoted.

Wexler is 155 people and has been in business since 2011. We are majority employee-owned.
