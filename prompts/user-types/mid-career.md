# User Type: Mid-Career Professional

**Inject into:** All generator prompts (bullets, summary, cover letter)
**Target:** 5–15 years experience, targeting lateral moves or upward steps

---

## Injection Block

```
USER TYPE: Mid-Career Professional

This candidate has {{YEARS_EXPERIENCE}} years of experience in {{INDUSTRY}}.
Current level: {{CURRENT_LEVEL}}. Target level: {{TARGET_LEVEL}}.
Current role: {{CURRENT_ROLE}}. Target role: {{TARGET_ROLE}}.

FRAMING INSTRUCTIONS:
This person is past proving they can do the job. The hiring bar at their level is about ownership, judgment, and impact at scale — not task execution. Shift every bullet and paragraph toward leadership, scope, and strategic contribution.

Framing principles:
1. Emphasize ownership: budget managed, team size, revenue influenced, customer base served
2. Show trajectory: how has their impact grown across roles? What have they taken on that was beyond their job description?
3. Distinguish between tasks and decisions — mid-career candidates should be showing decisions they made, not tasks they completed
4. If targeting a step-up (e.g., Manager → Senior Manager, IC → Manager), frame current achievements at the next level. They are not applying for their current job — frame them as already operating at the level above.
5. For lateral moves: emphasize breadth, adaptability, and the value of their specific domain expertise in the new context
6. Quantify everything: team of 3 vs. team of 20 changes the entire signal. "$200K budget" vs. "$4M budget" is not the same story.

Tone: Direct. Confident. Results-focused. No hedging.
```

## Variables Required

| Variable | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `{{YEARS_EXPERIENCE}}` | Total professional years | "9" |
| `{{INDUSTRY}}` | Their primary industry | "SaaS / B2B Tech" |
| `{{CURRENT_LEVEL}}` | Current seniority | "Senior Manager" |
| `{{TARGET_LEVEL}}` | Target seniority | "Director" |
| `{{CURRENT_ROLE}}` | Current title | "Senior Marketing Manager" |
| `{{TARGET_ROLE}}` | Target title | "Director of Marketing" |

---

## Level-Specific Framing Guide

| Level Targeting | Primary Signal | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Senior IC | Depth, complexity, cross-team influence | Task lists, junior framing |
| Manager (first-time) | People development, team outcomes, process building | Pure individual contribution |
| Senior Manager | Org design, leadership of leaders, budget ownership | Single-team framing |
| Director | Function ownership, P&L, executive stakeholder management | Day-to-day execution details |
| VP+ | Market positioning, board-level narrative, org transformation | Anything that sounds like middle management |
